Euro Area Monetary & Financial Conditions

How has ECB monetary policy been transmitted to money market rates, bank borrowing conditions and inflation in the euro area since 2015?

PROJECT SUMMARY

A reproducible transmission exercise

The project combines ECB and Eurostat data in a monthly dataset covering January 2015 to July 2026. It studies a simplified chain from the ECB Deposit Facility Rate to €STR, bank borrowing rates and inflation.

PythonPandasSQLiteECB APIEurostat API

KEY FINDINGS

What the data show

  • €STR tracks the ECB Deposit Facility Rate very closely over its available sample.
  • Monthly bank borrowing rate changes are most strongly associated with contemporaneous and recent DFR changes.
  • The contemporaneous full sample correlation is approximately 0.67 for household house purchase borrowing rates and 0.80 for corporate borrowing rates.
  • The relationship weakens substantially outside the 2022 to 2024 high adjustment period, especially for households.
  • Inflation correlations are descriptive and are not interpreted as causal monetary policy effects.
