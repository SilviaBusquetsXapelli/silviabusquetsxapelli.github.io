PROFILE

Making complex ideas easier to understand

I’m Silvia Busquets Xapellí. I am completing graduate studies across UCLouvain and Católica Lisbon School of Business & Economics, with a focus on economics and finance.

I built this website to turn what I study into explanations that are easier to understand, revisit and use. My interests sit at the intersection of economics, finance and European affairs, with a particular focus on the mechanisms behind how things work.

The Learning Hub brings these ideas together through concise lessons, questions and interactive material. I also create books and activities for different ages when a printable or longer format works better. Across both, the aim is the same: clear, structured and useful learning.
