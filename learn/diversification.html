What is diversification?

Diversification reduces unnecessary dependence on a single investment, company, sector or source of risk.

On this page
UnderstandExplorePracticeExplain itKey takeawaysSources

UNDERSTAND

Do not let one outcome decide everything

Holding different assets can reduce the damage caused by one specific investment performing badly. It cannot remove market risk or guarantee a profit.

Useful distinction

Ten holdings are not necessarily diversified if all ten respond to the same underlying risk. What matters is the mix of exposures, not just the count.

EXPLORE

PRACTICE

Check the idea

Which portfolio is more concentrated?

Common mistake: “Diversification means you cannot lose money.”

Diversification manages concentration risk; broad markets and many assets can still fall together.

EXPLAIN IT

60 second interview practice

“If I own ten different investments, am I definitely diversified?”

No. The number of holdings alone does not determine diversification. Ten investments can still depend on the same sector, country or underlying risk. Diversification is about spreading exposures, not simply counting positions.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Diversification reduces unnecessary dependence on a single investment, company, sector or source of risk.

Use the distinction above when you meet this concept in news, data or a real-world decision. The goal here is to understand the mechanism — not to memorise a definition.
