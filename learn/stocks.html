What is a stock?

A stock or share represents an ownership interest in a company.

What ownership means

Shareholders participate in the economic fortunes of the company. Some shares may pay dividends, but dividends are not guaranteed.

Why prices move

Prices respond to expectations about future profits, interest rates, risk and the price investors are willing to pay.

Risk

A share can rise or fall substantially and a company can fail. Ownership is not the same as a guaranteed bank deposit.
