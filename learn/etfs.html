What is an ETF?

An exchange-traded fund is a pooled investment vehicle whose shares trade on an exchange.

What it can hold

Depending on its mandate, an ETF can hold shares, bonds or other assets and may track an index or follow another strategy.

Diversification is not automatic

A broad-market ETF can spread exposure across many holdings; a narrow sector or single-theme ETF may still be highly concentrated.

Look beyond the label

Costs, index methodology, holdings, currency exposure, liquidity and structure can differ.
