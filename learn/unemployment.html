What is unemployment?

The unemployment rate describes people without work who are available for work and actively seeking it, under the statistical definition being used.

Not everyone without a job is unemployed

Students, retirees and people not currently seeking work are generally outside the labour force rather than counted as unemployed.

Why one number is not enough

Economists also look at employment rates, participation, hours worked, vacancies and different groups within the labour market.

Economic signal

Rising unemployment can indicate weak demand, but labour-market changes can also reflect technology, demographics and structural shifts.
